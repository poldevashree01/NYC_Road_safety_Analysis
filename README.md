# NYC_Traffic_ Collision_Records_Analysis

An interactive Power BI project analyzing **New York City motor vehicle collisions (2023–2025)** to uncover patterns, contributing factors, and high-risk locations.


## 📊 Project Overview
This dashboard explores collision data across NYC boroughs to identify:
- Key causes of road incidents  
- Yearly trends in collisions, injuries, and fatalities  
- Time and location-based risk patterns  
- Borough-level severity distribution  

The goal is to provide actionable insights for improving urban safety and traffic planning.


## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- **Trend Analysis:** Visualized 2023–2025 collision patterns, showing a sharp dip in 2024 followed by a rebound in 2025.  
- **Geospatial Insights:** Mapped collision hotspots across boroughs, with Brooklyn and Queens recording the highest incidents.  
- **Contributing Factors:** Identified top causes such as driver inattention, unsafe speed, and failure to yield.  
- **Severity & Timing:** Highlighted peak severity during evening hours (7–10 PM) and highest collisions on Fridays.


## 🧠 Key Insights
- Collisions **peaked in 2023 (89K)**, dropped in 2024 (62K), and rose again in 2025 (84K).  
- **Brooklyn and Queens** accounted for the majority of collisions.  
- **Driver inattention** remains the leading contributing factor.  
- **Fridays** see the highest number of incidents, while **Sundays** are lowest.  
- **Severity** tends to increase in the **evening hours (7–10 PM)**.


## 🧰 Tools & Skills
- **SQL** – Data extraction and cleaning from NYC collision datasets  
- **Snowflake** – Data modeling and aggregation for analysis  
- **Power BI** – Visualization and dashboard creation  
- **DAX** – Custom KPIs, measures, and time intelligence
